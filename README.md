# Skills Wiki (self-hosted)

**Manage the AI skills you already installed.** Skills Wiki routes each request to the right skill, remembers
your corrections as learnings, and evaluates and benchmarks your skills on your own tokens. Everything runs
on your machine. Optionally, bring your own [TypeSafe](https://typesafe.ai) key and JEV, a calibrated decision
model, does the routing, checks your eval suites and grades through a cascade.

> **Status: under construction.** The first usable version (v0.1) is being built in the open, here.

Works with skills for Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI (folders containing a `SKILL.md`).
